SCENE

INT. THE ARCHITECTURE OF REGRET - TIMELESS

A vast, internal coldness dominates the foreground—a deep, depressive cobalt stagnancy where ambition once stood. But on the periphery, a manic, amber violence erupts. It is the burning of bridges, a chaotic illumination that offers heat but no warmth.

The floor of the mind is carpeted in the sharp, granular debris of old arguments. Thousands of tiny, jagged resentments vibrate in unison, a nervous tremor rippling through the foundation of the self.

A single distillation of pure, unadulterated Truth—clear and terrifying—descends from the void.

It strikes the fevered callous of the Ego.

Contact.

There is no acceptance, only immediate, violent sublimation. The Truth is instantly boiled away, transforming into a thick, white ribbon of Denial. This vapor rises, distorting the perception of reality, bending the air into heat-haze hallucinations of what could have been.

Then, the pressure drops. A sudden, suffocating silence.

A heavy, velvet grief begins to fall like ash. It settles over the burned-out landscape of memory, muting the noise, burying the sharp edges under a soft, grey suffocation.

The foundation gives way. A fissure tears open through the center of the consciousness. It is a silent rupture. From deep within the abyss of the identity, a soft, steady glow emits—the dangerous, seductive light of total dissociation.

EXT. REFINERY GROUNDS - NIGHT

Amber light flares intermittently in the background, bleaching out the deep blue shadows of the foreground.

A heavy lead plate sits on a rusted steel table. The plate vibrates with a low mechanical hum. On its surface, mounds of jagged iron filings and coarse black grit jitter and shift into geometric patterns.

A single droplet of clear oil falls from an unseen source above.

It strikes the hot grit.

HISS.

The oil instantly boils. Thick, white ribbons of chemical vapor shoot upward. The air above the plate warps—heat distortion ripples the view of the background flares.

The vibration stops abruptly.

A concussive wave of air snaps through the yard, kicking up dust, but makes no sound.

Grey flakes of ash drift down, landing on the uneven surface of sun-baked asphalt.

In the center of the pavement, the ground splits. A fissure runs three feet long, then widens.

A steady, soft orange glow shines up from the interior of the crack.
